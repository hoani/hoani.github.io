Explore the world of Final Fantasy I in a way you never have before, through the single-player rouge-like playing card game Scoundrel.

Play as your favorite warrior of light. Each class has their own buffs and item set, ranging from easy to hard.

This game is a love letter to both Final Fantasy I and Scoundrel, both single player dungeon adventure games which I deeply love.
