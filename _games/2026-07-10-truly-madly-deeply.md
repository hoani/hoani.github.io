Yearning for a simpler life, Marceline takes out her life savings, hands in her resignation, and pours everything she has into an old, abandoned mine located on the outskirts of her childhood hometown.

Will she follow her heart’s true path? Descend into immortal madness? Or fall, deeply, for the very first time?
