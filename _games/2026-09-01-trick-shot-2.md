The Bone King has blackened the Sun. The Sun Stones must be purified by the Snake Deity Kaj. Return the orbs to Kaj and undo the Bone King's evil deeds.

Trick Shot II is a fun physics puzzler built to feel like a Nokia 3310 game you might play in class in 2002.

**About**

Trick Shot II placed 1st overall and in all categories in the itch.io [Nokia 3310 Jam 8](https://itch.io/jam/nokiajam8). 

The theme of the Jam was `Luck's got nothin' to do with it`. So I made a game where you try to get a ball to bounce towards a pot.



I wanted to make the game as close to the original Nokia 3310 as possible. I built a barebones game engine on top of `tinygo` and `ebitengine`, which could build games to run on a microcontroller, desktop and browser.

## Awards

Trick Shot II [placed 1st overall](https://itch.io/jam/nokiajam8/results) in the Nokia 3310 Jam 8. It ranked first in all three categories for Innovation, Completeness and Quality.
